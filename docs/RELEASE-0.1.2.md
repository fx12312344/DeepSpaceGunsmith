# Deep Space Gunsmith 0.1.2

## 中文

### 扩选材料，一次投入
- CNC与弹药制造台支持扩选多份独立材料后，一起拖到机器图标投入；原有单件投料保留。
- 不同纯度金属逐件换算；容量不足时，只接收能容纳的份数，其余留在原位置。
- 堆叠材料仍需先拆开，防止原版拆分丢失纯度。材料兑换、5000储量上限、配方与耗电不变。

### 更平滑的商品收益
- 调整52支枪、36件改装配件、22件供弹件的基础价值，采用由低价30%向顶级75%平滑增长的目标成本加成曲线。
- 采购报价按固定旧价参考限制，避免进货价随新售价同比上涨；常规枪单参考奖励接入对应曲线。
- 实际收益仍受品相、市场、议价、批发和制造成本影响；最佳订单与顾客服务订单沿用原版结算。
- 弹药、材料、机器、容器价格及生产消耗保持。旧存档中已有物品可能保留旧价，新生成物品使用新价。

### 枪架与机器外观
- 小、中、大枪架换为无灯、无发光装饰的旧工业风格，并保留原占格和容量。
- 收纳物品使用矩形点击区域，枪架空隙也可点选。
- 更新弹药制造台高清贴图，等比适配原3×5占格；CNC保持6×4。

## English

### Group-select materials and deposit them together
- Both workbenches now accept multiple separate material items selected as a group and dropped onto the machine icon. Single-item deposits remain available.
- Metal purity is evaluated per item. When reserves are nearly full, only items that fit are consumed; the rest remain in their original inventory.
- Split stacks before depositing to preserve purity. Conversion rates, 5,000-point reserve caps, recipes and power costs are unchanged.

### A smoother trading profit curve
- Rebalanced base values for 52 firearms, 36 attachments and 22 feed components using a target cost-markup curve rising smoothly from 30% for low-price goods to 75% for top-price goods.
- Procurement quotes use fixed pre-update reference caps rather than rising in step with the new sale values. Regular firearm-order reference rewards use the corresponding curve.
- Actual profit still depends on condition, market effects, bargaining, wholesale discounts and production costs. Best-gun and customer service orders retain vanilla settlement rules.
- Ammunition, material, machine and container prices, and crafting costs, remain unchanged. Existing saved items may retain old prices; newly generated items use the new values.

### Storage and machine artwork
- Replaced all three gun-rack images with unlit, worn industrial designs while preserving grid sizes and capacities.
- Dedicated storage uses rectangular hit regions, including empty space between rack supports.
- Updated the high-resolution ammunition-workbench artwork, fitted proportionally inside its existing 3×5 grid. The CNC remains 6×4.
