# Deep Space Gunsmith — Version 0.1.0 Update
## New Firearms, New Workshops, and New Supply Channels

This update expands firearm collecting and customization while bringing production, purchasing, and storage features together in the main mod.

### New Firearms and Attachments
- Added **11 real-world firearms**, including the TT-33, SVT-40, SKS, M4A1, and MP 40.
- Added the **BFG 9000** from DOOM, classified as a sniper rifle for gameplay, with higher stats and a higher price than the Barrett M82A1.
- Added **36 attachments**, covering optics, stocks, grips, muzzle devices, trigger groups, plating kits, and firearm-specific modifications.
- Expanded firearm seller inventories and the pool of firearms available for customer modification orders.

### New Production Systems

**Gunsmith’s Multipurpose CNC Workbench**
- Replaces the previous workbench and supports 64 recipes, material reserves, batch manufacturing, and overnight production.
- **Each individual material item deposited adds the following amount to its corresponding reserve:**

| Material | Reserve Added |
|---|---:|
| Metal ingot: Very Low / Low / Fair purity | 75 / 85 / 100 metal |
| Metal ingot: High / Very High / Perfect purity | 125 / 150 / 300 metal |
| Common electronic component | **25 electronics** |
| 3D printer material | **25 printer material** |
| Nuclear waste | **500 nuclear waste** |

**Ammunition Workbench**
- Supports 23 ammunition recipes. Each batch takes one working night and uses 8 power in total.
- **Each individual material item deposited adds the following amount to its corresponding reserve:**

| Material | Reserve Added |
|---|---:|
| Smokeless Powder and Primers | **100 powder and primers** |
| Pile of Cartridge Cases | **100 cartridge cases** |
| Pile of Projectiles | **100 projectiles** |
| Nuclear waste | **500 nuclear waste** |

Both machines have a **separate 5,000-point cap for each reserve**. Materials must be owned by the player and deposited individually; split stacks before depositing. Deposits that would exceed the cap are refused. Nuclear waste is stored as a sealed reserve and is not consumed by current recipes. Both machines pause when power is insufficient, retain pending products when the output is full, and support continuous output.

### Wholesale and Supply Services
- Integrated the **Firearms Wholesaler**, offering bulk purchases of firearms, feed components, and ammunition.
- Added **Peace Margin Industries**, a telephone supply service with small and large orders, next-day delivery, shareholder discounts, and free dividend deliveries.
- Both machines are available through the inventor’s stock.

### Balance and Quality-of-Life Improvements
- Reduced material requirements for most CNC recipes and all ammunition recipes, and adjusted supplier prices.
- Increased the reserve gained from each common electronic component and 3D printer material item to **25 points**.
- Integrated dedicated gun racks, a gun parts box, and an ammunition box; refined sprite scaling, firearm orientation, and some attachment visuals.
- Fixed issues involving duplicate production when stacking, repeated power consumption, batch output, and supplier spending calculations.

### Chinese and English Text Overhaul
- Completed Chinese and English names and game-style descriptions for all added items.
- Removed development labels such as “Test” from item names and completed missing Chinese firearm names.
- Both machines now include operating instructions and material-to-reserve conversion rates in their descriptions.

**Update Notes:** Do not load the standalone workbench, ammunition machine, or supplier mods alongside this integrated version. The old workbench has no save migration; keep machines and containers holding them off the customer counter. After changing the game language, reload your save to refresh existing item text.

**Thank you for your support. May your business prosper and your collection keep growing!**
