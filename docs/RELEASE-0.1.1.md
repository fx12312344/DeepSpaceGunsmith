# Deep Space Gunsmith 0.1.1

## 中文 · 库存贴图适配更新

- 全量核对52支枪械、36件改装配件及22件供弹件，共110件物品的贴图、占格和显示比例。
- 优化9支枪和9件供弹件的透明留白，让图案更贴合库存格子；SVT-40、M1903和MP 40的显示大小改善最明显。
- 保持原占格、高清画布和长宽比例，不拉伸枪身、不裁掉主体。
- 同步调整9支枪的配件装配锚点，配件本体大小不变；保护原有枪托及镀层资源。
- 36件改装配件已符合合理填充比例，保留原图，避免重复采样。
- 不改变物品价格、属性、生产配方或材料兑换量。

## English · Inventory Artwork Fit Update

- Audited inventory artwork, grid dimensions and display scale for 52 firearms, 36 attachments and 22 feed components: 110 items in total.
- Reduced excessive transparent padding on 9 firearms and 9 feed components, making their artwork better fill the existing inventory cells. The SVT-40, M1903 and MP 40 see the most noticeable improvements.
- Preserved grid sizes, high-resolution canvases and aspect ratios, without stretching weapons or cropping their silhouettes.
- Remapped attachment mounting anchors on the 9 adjusted firearms while keeping attachment sizes unchanged; existing stock and plating resources remain protected.
- Left the 36 well-fitted attachment inventory icons unchanged to avoid unnecessary resampling.
- No changes to prices, stats, crafting recipes or material conversion rates.
